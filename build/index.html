Founding Member of Technical Staff @ Axiom

Foundation Models for Biology, Autonomy & Healthcare

I am a Founding Member of Technical Staff at Axiom, where I build foundation models for biology, autonomy and healthcare — cellular phenotyping and drug discovery in particular. I completed my MS in Computer Science at UMass Amherst (CICS) and my undergraduate degree at IIT Delhi, where I focused on theoretical machine learning and natural language processing.

Most recently I have been using high-content microscopy images to learn what toxic presentations in cells look like, and using those learned representations to predict toxic structures in drug molecules. More broadly, I am interested in multimodal models for biology, and in the calibration and robustness of models used for high-stakes decisions.
